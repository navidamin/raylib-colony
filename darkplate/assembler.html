Reading it

Amber is machinery and attention, cyan is instrument and information, hot is heat and damage. The lamp goes cyan → amber → hot as the machine is driven, and the housing warms through the material itself, never under a tint layer.

The doorway's bright inner edge is the amber plate's own thickness, painted in plating steel: the frame is a skin over a steel machine, and a cut is where you find that out.
