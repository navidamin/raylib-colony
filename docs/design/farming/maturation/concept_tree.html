ROOT

Farming Unit — turns light, water, regolith and biology into calories, oxygen and morale — and pilots the uniform definition of a unit. Bound by the three project principles: scientific coherence · multi-scale control · progressive disclosure.

A

CHARTER — the uniform unit definition

●
  • A1●
    Field grammar — terms that survive all eight unit types
    • A1.adraws vs gates — consumed rates vs held thresholds
    • A1.bstocks vs services — Transport breaks naive uniformity ○
    • A1.ccycle — duration + phases on TimeManager ticks
    • A1.dmedium — the generalized “type of planting”
    • A1.ecouplings — the “Look at” gauges as a field
  • A2◆
    Naming decided — UnitDescriptor { draws · gates · yields · cycle · medium · couplings } + CropDescriptor, on the ResourceDescriptor precedent
  • A3●
    Data-driven catalog — “128 seeds” as CropDescriptor rows in game_types.toml; register sizes n∈{4,14,16,24,128} are views, not data
  • A4○
    Migration path — farming pilots → service-yield hard cases → extraction retrofit last
  • A5○
    FOOD goes TYPED — subtypes (grains · greens · protein · fungi) so variety, morale and the register have grip
B

MEDIA — cultivation substrates

●
  • B1●
    SOIL — regolith agriculture
    • B1.aregolith inputs from beneficiation — REGOLITH · MEGAREGOLITH · FRACTURED · BASALT
    • B1.bconditioning & per-plot fertility — a real degrade/restore loop (fixes decay-to-floor)
    • B1.cprofile — low power, high mass & time, fails soft
  • B2◆
    HYDRO — solution culture  → B2-hydroponics.md
    • B2.atechnique family — DWC · NFT · EBB · DRIP, ranked by failure latency
    • B2.bsolution chemistry — EC & pH drift caused by the plants; N from the crew loop, K+P from KREEP, micros with Al/Cr removed
    • B2.croot zone — dissolved O₂ is the real limiter; capillary rise ×6 at 0.16 g → SIZE_SORT is the substrate mill
    • B2.dlight regime — PPFD/DLI; the spectrum tax paid in watts for crew eyes
    • B2.eatmosphere — CO₂ zoning vs crew access; ethylene; the farm as water purifier
    • B2.ffailure latency — hours from power loss to first death; buffer is mass
  • B3●
    Medium roster — 13 media × 2 axes: trophic route × loop closed → B3-media-roster.md
    • B3.aphototrophic — SOIL · SUBSTRATE · HYDRO · AERO · AQUATIC
    • B3.bchemotrophic — VAT (watts→calories, lightless) · LITHO (→F5)
    • B3.cheterotrophic — MYCO · INSECT · AQUA (closes N) · CULTURE
    • B3.dspecial-loop — SPROUT · HALO (eats brine) · PIONEER (makes soil)
    • B3.eship order — core five: SOIL · SUBSTRATE · HYDRO · MYCO · VAT ◆
  • B4●
    Trade-off table — growth rate × failure latency; the roster collapses onto one line
  • B5◆
    Portfolio rule — every latency is measured against the same failure: power. Diversity is computable insurance; lightless families survive the night. Never one medium.
  • B6◆
    Microbial ceiling — nucleic-acid load caps SCP near 20–25 g/day. The most efficient food cannot feed the crew alone — biology, not balance
C

REGISTER — the implicit farming algorithm

●
  • C1●
    The invariant — Eulerian rotation over crop pairs; siting only instantiates (n, tiers, service rate)
  • C2●
    Constraint → program map — PHAROS · FORTNIGHT · DEEP REGISTER · ASHFIELD · PILGRIM · VAULT bound to SiteArchetypes
  • C3◆
    Implicitness principle — never homework; UI shows rhythm & health (C3.a ○ — how the nudge works)
  • C4○
    Time binding — 20 ticks/day vs the 29.5-day synodic month; does the planet get a real light cycle? Biggest engine ask.
  • C5○
    Recognition & reward — the game names your settled rhythm; grand cycles as long-horizon milestones
