Lunar Medium Map

Thirteen cultivation media, placed on the axis that actually decides between them. Colour is the trophic route — where the energy enters the food. Hover any medium for what it eats, what loop it closes, and its real-world basis.

Every medium buys speed with buffer. Water you carry is time you have when the pump stops. That single trade puts the whole roster on one line — and because every latency is measured against the same failure (power), spreading a farm across media is not flavour, it is computable insurance.

The ceiling that keeps the roster honest

Microbial protein carries a high nucleic-acid load; human uric-acid handling caps intake near 20–25 g/day. So the most energy-efficient food source on this map — VAT, which turns watts into calories with no photosynthesis in the path — cannot feed a crew alone. Not a balance decision: biology. Diet diversity is forced, and "why not just build VATs" answers itself.
