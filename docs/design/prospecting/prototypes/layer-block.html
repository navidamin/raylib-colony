Prospecting · block model · solid-body study

Layer Block

The four exploded plates, rebuilt as one solid body that is cut into four strata. The drill bar is unchanged and still shares the column's depth axis — now a straight one, so the bands are at last their true thickness. Click a layer (on the block or on the chips) and everything above it is taken off: the layer is left translucent, so you read its surface and the floor beneath it at the same time. Click the ground outside the block, or press Escape, to put it back.

N=32 · 0–120 M

What the block gives up, and what it buys. Exploded, all four class maps were readable at once; solid, only the surface is — that is the cost, and the peel is what pays it back. What it buys: one body instead of four floating sheets, layer thickness that is finally to scale, a depth axis with no gaps to interpolate across, and a floor to put the class map on where nothing is standing on top of it.
