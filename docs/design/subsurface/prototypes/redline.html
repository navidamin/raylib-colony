The ladder, worst first. Heat in the ice-bearing fractured zone sublimates the water out of the core, so that interval assays falsely low — and nothing tells you at the time. Driving past the band grinds the core and leaves a gap in the column where you most wanted a number. A dull bit is the cheapest failure: it costs a trip, and a trip costs time proportional to how deep you already are.

Which is the whole push-your-luck: bit at 60% at 40 m — trip now while it is cheap, or push on and risk tripping from 90 m?
