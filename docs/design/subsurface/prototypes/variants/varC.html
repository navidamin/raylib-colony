Reading it

Heat climbs with RPM × hardness and falls when you dwell. Past the red line the steel cracks — and in the fractured layer, the ice you came for sublimates out of the core.

The depth ticker runs quick in soft ground, crawls in basalt, and jumps at a fracture — the drill is an instrument even when the core is lost.
