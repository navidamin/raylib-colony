Regolith & Craters

The Layer Block's bowl, put into the ground the game actually renders. This is src/TerrainGen/terrain_synthesis.cpp ported to JS: real LROC WAC imagery, cropped, sharpened, re-read as relief, grained and lit, with a configurable crater population carved into the height field before the sun reaches it. Left is the mosaic itself — the actual texels the synthesizer had — and right is what it made of them, so at every zoom you can see exactly how much is data and how much is invention. Drag or click to move the window, scroll to zoom, Shift-click to drop the selected crater, Esc to re-centre on the region.
