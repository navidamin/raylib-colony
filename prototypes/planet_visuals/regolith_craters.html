Regolith & Craters

The Layer Block's bowl, put into the ground the game actually renders. This is src/TerrainGen/terrain_synthesis.cpp ported to JS — the real LROC WAC mosaic around Plinius (15.4°N, 23.7°E), cropped, sharpened, re-read as relief, grained and lit — running its own 100 → 25 → 5 km chain, with a configurable crater population carved into the height field before the sun reaches it. Craters are pinned to the moon in km, so one crater is the same object at all three scales. Click a panel to move the selected crater there; Esc deselects.
